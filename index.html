Our Story

Rooted in nature.
Designed for stillness.

High in the Palani Hills, Shrishti Gardens is a boutique nature retreat in Kodaikanal — a small collection of thirteen rooms set apart from the everyday, where cool mountain air and green surroundings set the pace of the day.

We keep things intimate and unhurried. Thoughtful hospitality, comfortable suites and a sense of privacy let you slow down, rest well and enjoy the quieter side of the hill station.
